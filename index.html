Squirm Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · App: Squirm (com.codeenzyme.squirm) · Developer: Code Enzyme

Squirm is a burrowing worm puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information is handled when you play it, why, and the choices you have. The short version: the game itself does not ask for, collect or send any personal information. Purchases go through Google Play, and the optional reward videos come from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to serve them.

Information the game stores on your device

Squirm saves your level progress, seeds, hints, a record of what you've bought, and your sound and control settings in the app's private storage on your phone. This information never leaves your device except through Android's own app backup, if you have turned that on in your device settings. Squirm has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own.

Purchases (Google Play)

Full Burrow and seed packs are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and Code Enzyme never sees your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game when a purchase is complete so it can give you the item, and keeps a record of your purchases on your Google account so Full Burrow can be restored if you reinstall. Google's handling of purchases is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Squirm can offer short video ads that you choose to watch for a reward, such as extra seeds. They are provided by Google AdMob and only play when you tap to watch one. When a video loads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information from your device, which may include:

InformationUsed for
Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set IDShowing ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud, and (where you allow it) personalised ads
IP address and approximate location derived from itShowing ads relevant to your region, fraud prevention, and analytics
App interactions with ads, such as ad views and tapsMeasuring and improving ads, and fraud prevention
Diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data of the ads SDKKeeping ads working correctly

This information is shared with Google, which acts as the advertising provider. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad loads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy options button in the game's settings.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings under Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads on some phones).
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling Squirm deletes all the progress and settings it stored on your device. For data held by Google for purchases or advertising, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Squirm is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
